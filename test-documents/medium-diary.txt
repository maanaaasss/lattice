I was in my first year of my diploma, and everything was new since I had spent my first year online. One day, I was going through my college group, and a notification popped up for an internship. The pitch was almost funny. It was essentially a setup to sell courses to get incentives, get promoted to team leader, and then make more money based on the team's performance. In short, a salesman. I thought, why not try it? So, I joined.
I joined under a team leader. I think her name was Shruti. I was under her for a month and didn't make any sales, which was obvious. Eventually, I got transferred to a different team. This time I had two team leaders, a girl and a guy who turned out to be a couple. Their names were Vanshika and Shivam. They were pretty cool, and since everything was new to me, I was fascinated by the environment.
Then, I got introduced to a new girl named Mink. She was also a team lead. We were in an informal meeting together, and I found out she was studying finance and law. During that meet, I cracked a joke, saying that since she was a lawyer, I would prefer to stay shut because she could put me in jail. After that, we didn't speak. A few days later, I was transferred to work under her team. I had to text her for work, and it turned out I was blocked.
We eventually started talking about work and other things. Some days later, I was feeling tense about my family situation, and she asked me what was up. I told her, and she related to it, saying she had a similarly tough situation at home. That was the turning point where we actually connected. I decided to leave the internship. After almost 5 to 6 months of working and making zero sales, I walked away.
By that point, Mink and I were close. We talked about everything. She mentioned that she had also left the internship, but she had earned the company almost 1M in total revenue, which blew my mind. We were both out of the internship and growing emotionally attached. It is probably more accurate to say it was an emotionally dependent bond. We both realized it was turning into something more. The relationship officially started in December 2021.
She was originally from Jharkhand but was studying in Kolkata. After a few months, she decided she wanted to come to Nagpur to meet me. I was excited and nervous that someone was coming all the way from Kolkata just for me. We planned everything out, and she booked her tickets for April 1, 2022. Her flight was arriving at 10:45 PM, so I had to figure out a way to tell my parents I would be out.
When her flight landed, I picked her up, and we decided to stay at the airport for a while before going to the hotel. The situation was tricky. I was 17, so legally I couldn't book tickets or a room in my name. I asked a friend to book a room in the same hotel. Mink and I stayed at the airport until 3:45 AM, then booked a taxi to the hotel and spent the rest of the night there.
Later, we decided to go watch a movie. It was RRR, and we didn't watch a single minute of it because we were so exhausted from staying up all night. After the movie, my friend Sujit joined us, and we went bowling. We bought bread and jam and decided to just stay in the metro station on the way to the airport.
Around 5:30 AM, we left for the airport. We sat there for a while, talking and realizing how emotionally attached we were. Then it was time for her to go inside. I started walking back. That day, I walked almost 12KM without even realizing it. That was the first time in my life I felt that I belonged somewhere.
