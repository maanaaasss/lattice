I was in my first year of my diploma, and everything was new since I had spent my first year online. One day, I was going through my college group, and a notification popped up for an internship. The pitch was almost funny. It was essentially a setup to sell courses to get incentives, get promoted to team leader, and then make more money based on the team's performance. In short, a salesman. I laugh looking back at it, especially considering what I was reading about my kundli earlier today. I thought, why not try it? I was getting bored doing nothing at home anyway. So, I joined. It was nothing fancy. They just called, asked if I had any experience talking to people, I said no, and they hired me.
I joined under a team leader. I think her name was Shruti. I was under her for a month and didn't make any sales, which was obvious. Eventually, I got transferred to a different team. This time I had two team leaders, a girl and a guy who turned out to be a couple. Their names were Vanshika and Shivam. They were pretty cool, and since everything was new to me, I was fascinated by the environment. I got a little close with both of them and a few other teammates. By this point, almost three months had passed, and the situation at my house was deteriorating quickly. I had a lot of issues at home and used to have frequent fights with my mother and siblings.
Then, I got introduced to a new girl named Mink. She was also a team lead. We were in an informal meeting together, and I found out she was studying finance and law. During that meet, I cracked a joke, saying that since she was a lawyer, I would prefer to stay shut because she could put me in jail. After that, we didn't speak. A few days later, I was transferred to work under her team. I had to text her for work, and it turned out I was blocked. I tagged her in the group asking for clarity. She unblocked me and said she didn't like my joke, so she had just blocked me. I just thought it was funny.
We eventually started talking about work and other things. Some days later, I was feeling tense about my family situation, and she asked me what was up. I told her, and she related to it, saying she had a similarly tough situation at home. That was the turning point where we actually connected. We started talking about our struggles and how difficult things were. One day, my sister checked my phone. I don't know why, maybe because my family always hated the kind of internship I was doing since it was completely different from my domain. I was really only doing it because I had made good friends there. I decided to leave the internship. After almost 5 to 6 months of working and making zero sales, I walked away.
By that point, Mink and I were close. We talked about everything. She mentioned that she had also left the internship, but she had earned the company almost 1M in total revenue, which blew my mind. I think this was around July. We were both out of the internship and growing emotionally attached. It is probably more accurate to say it was an emotionally dependent bond. We both realized it was turning into something more. In August, we started talking the way couples do. We were just emotionally dependent on each other, and this kept going until December when we proposed to each other. The relationship officially started in December 2021.
She was originally from Jharkhand but was studying in Kolkata. After a few months, she decided she wanted to come to Nagpur to meet me. I was excited and nervous that someone was coming all the way from Kolkata just for me. We planned everything out, and she booked her tickets for April 1, 2022. Her flight was arriving at 10:45 PM, so I had to figure out a way to tell my parents I would be out. I came up with an excuse that there were college sports going on and I needed to stay there for three days. They bought it.
When her flight landed, I picked her up, and we decided to stay at the airport for a while before going to the hotel. The situation was tricky. I was 17, so legally I couldn't book tickets or a room in my name, and there was no chance I could live with her in the same room. I asked a friend to book a room in the same hotel and asked him to stay with me there for one night. Mink and I stayed at the airport until 3:45 AM, then booked a taxi to the hotel and spent the rest of the night there. Around 7 or 8 AM, she called me so we could go out for breakfast. We checked out right after because we had only booked for one day.
Later, we decided to go watch a movie. It was RRR, and we didn't watch a single minute of it because we were so exhausted from staying up all night. After the movie, my friend Sujit joined us, and we went bowling. It was almost evening, and her return flight was early the next morning. Since we didn't have living arrangements, we bought bread and jam and decided to just stay in the metro station on the way to the airport. A guard asked what we were doing there so late, so we explained her early flight. He allowed us to stay and waited nearby just in case we needed help.
Around 5:30 AM, we left for the airport, which was about a 1KM walk. We sat there for a while, talking and realizing how emotionally attached we were. Then it was time for her to go inside. I started walking back. That day, I walked almost 12KM without even realizing it. That was the first time in my life I felt that I belonged somewhere, or knew that someone in the world actually cared for me. During that time, my living situation was bad, and I didn't know I had so many underlying problems. When I got home, my mother was furious and gave me the silent treatment again. After that trip, the bond between Mink and I deepened. It felt very pure.
We kept talking and learning more about each other. We became very aware of each other's patterns and mental states. A year later, on March 23, 2023, we planned another trip to Nagpur. I was smarter and more confident this time. She came by train, arriving at 3:45 AM. I had everything sorted with the help of two friends. We went to a much better hotel and booked just one room. We fell asleep immediately and woke up around 12 PM. We had a light brunch, went to a nearby cafe for a date in the evening, and returned to the hotel later that night. We talked and cried for a while because our family situations were still so awful. We slept it off because we had an early trip planned the next day with my friends to a river on the outskirts of Nagpur.
We spent the entire day at the river and headed back to the hotel at night. We had a light dinner and talked about our future. She had an early morning flight, so we checked out the next morning and went to the airport. I stayed there and we said our goodbyes. Then, I started my long walk home. Goodbyes always feel heavy because I always fear I might not get to see the person again.
After that, she got a job at EY, a top finance firm. We were happy, but then my issues started appearing. My depression started growing, and I didn't even know what it was back then. I was skeptical about mental health issues. I started experiencing anxiety, depression, and a meaningless rage. I didn't know why it was happening. Mink was doing better with her mental health and career, but she still had her own issues. She tried her best to help me through it, but I turned on her. I dropped all my stress and anger onto her and started taking her for granted. She kept trying to make things right. Once I calmed down, I would try to work on myself, but it was difficult because I didn't understand my own suffering, and my parents were constantly blaming me for everything.
I should note that when we were dating, she was 20 and I was 17. A few months later, I left for Sangli. We were both excited that I was finally out of my house. But after getting to Sangli, things took a drastic turn. It got worse between us, and she just couldn't take it anymore. She had tried enough, but my behavior wasn't getting any better. One day, she decided to break it off and leave. I was furious and in denial. While my life was falling apart, she was my only source of sanity. But looking back, I realize anyone would have done the same thing. Another girl might have left long before she did.
Things got much worse in Sangli after that, which I have already documented in previous entries. I used to think my suffering was because of our separation. That was part of it, but the real issue was the trauma I had accumulated since childhood. After the breakup, she blocked me on WhatsApp. I texted her once or twice for clarity, but it never happened. In mid-2024, she texted me, but I was still furious with my life and turned my anger onto her. That was my last mistake, because we never spoke after that.
Months later, I finally gained clarity on a lot of things, especially this relationship. I wanted to reach out and talk to her like a real person, like the friend I met in that internship, and tell her she was right. There was no way for her to end things smoothly because I wouldn't have let it happen. She had no choice but to leave abruptly. I never got the chance to see her in Bangalore. A year ago, I saw her LinkedIn profile and noticed she was living there. I don't have any harsh feelings toward her anymore. What happened, had to happen. I just feel sometimes that I should talk to her before anything bad happens to me, because she deserves to know.
In my other journal entries, I noted that Mink, Purti, and Samruddhi had a huge impact on my life. I am still figuring out the last two, but Mink's impact was significant. She taught me a lot without even knowing it, and I respect her deeply. I am glad it all happened.
